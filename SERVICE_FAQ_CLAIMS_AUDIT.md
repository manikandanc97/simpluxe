## Owner decisions needed before replacement

- Confirm whether any delivery ranges or sprint cadence are standard contractual commitments.
- Confirm post-launch support coverage and whether maintenance is sold under separate terms.
- Confirm code, design-asset and intellectual-property transfer language with the project agreement.
- Confirm which named payment, SSO, ERP, hosting and app-store services are currently supported.
- Provide evidence and test conditions before publishing concurrency, Core Web Vitals, security or compliance outcomes.
